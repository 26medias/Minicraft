## Changes

All tiles were modified: cropped to frame 0 of any animation, resized to 16×16 where needed, and their alpha
normalised to the game's block flags (forced opaque, snapped to 0/255, or kept). Some tiles were greyscaled and
tinted, and some were composited from two sources. Grass tops and some leaves are tinted when the atlas is built.
Derived and composited tiles:

- copper_ore: bauniclonia mineclonia/ITEMS/mcl_copper/mcl_copper_ore.png, composited over stone
- creaking_heart_awake: bauniclonia mineclonia/ITEMS/mcl_pale_oak/mcl_pale_oak_log.png, greyscaled and tinted
- creaking_heart_top_awake: bauniclonia mineclonia/ITEMS/mcl_pale_oak/mcl_pale_oak_log_top.png, greyscaled and tinted
- muddy_mangrove_roots_side: bauniclonia mineclonia/ITEMS/mcl_mangrove/mcl_mangrove_roots_side.png, composited over mud
- muddy_mangrove_roots_top: bauniclonia mineclonia/ITEMS/mcl_mangrove/mcl_mangrove_roots_top.png, composited over mud
- ochre_froglight_side: ppce assets/minecraft/textures/block/shroomlight.png, greyscaled and tinted
- pearlescent_froglight_side: ppce assets/minecraft/textures/block/shroomlight.png, greyscaled and tinted
- suspicious_gravel_0: mineclonia mods/ITEMS/mcl_sus_nodes/textures/mcl_sus_nodes_suspicious_overlay.png, composited over gravel
- suspicious_sand_0: mineclonia mods/ITEMS/mcl_sus_nodes/textures/mcl_sus_nodes_suspicious_overlay.png, composited over sand
- tnt_bottom: ppce assets/minecraft/textures/block/tnt_top1.png, greyscaled and tinted
- tnt_side: ppce assets/minecraft/textures/block/tnt_side2.png, greyscaled and tinted
- tnt_top: ppce assets/minecraft/textures/block/tnt_top2.png, greyscaled and tinted
- verdant_froglight_side: ppce assets/minecraft/textures/block/shroomlight.png, greyscaled and tinted
